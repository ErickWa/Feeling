## 2026-11-20: No well-being or health questions in China-based interviews

- **Decision:** Oral histories in China stay on work, feelings and outlook. No health, mental-health or physiological data.
- **Why:** Under PIPL, sensitive personal information needs extra safeguards for cross-border transfer, and there's no institutional legal support yet.
- **Alternatives considered:** Collect it with consent alone (rejected: legal risk); delay all China interviews (rejected: loses the comparison).
- **Affects:** Track 2, Form 3 (point 9), Wave 0 design.
