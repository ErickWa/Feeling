# Panel invitations (EN drafts)

Drafts for inviting people to the contact panel by email and LinkedIn, each
with a request to pass the invitation on. Everything here repeats what the
home page and privacy policy already say. If those pages change, check these
drafts too.

**Referrals: ask people to pass the link on, not to send you names.** Someone
who forwards an invitation lets their friend decide whether to get in touch.
Someone who sends you a colleague's name and email gives you personal data
about a person who agreed to nothing, and that data then has to be logged,
kept and deleted like any other (CLAUDE.md invariant 1). Every draft below
asks people to forward the invitation.

**Every draft says what the project will never ask for.** The domain was
registered on 2026-11-20, and to someone at an AI lab a brand-new site asking
for a name and email is what phishing looks like. Naming the only domain, and
what a fake would ask for, lets them check before they click; the home page
FAQ and the site's 404 page say the same.

**Send from hello@feelingthemachine.com where you can.** Replies then reach
the address the privacy policy names. LinkedIn messages come from your
personal profile anyway.

Fill in the `[brackets]` and delete any you don't use.

## 1. Email: personal invitation

**Subject:** An invitation: what does it feel like to build AI?

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm starting a long-term oral history project, *Feeling the Machine*, about
> how people who build AI experience its growing abilities: the wonder, the
> unease, and what it does to how they see themselves. [I thought of you
> because …] I'd like to invite you to join its contact panel.
>
> Joining takes about five minutes at https://feelingthemachine.com/account.
> It only means you agree that I may contact you about future conversations
> and studies. Nothing else is asked of you, and you can leave at any time.
> Your name and email address are encrypted in your browser before they are
> sent, so the website itself cannot read them. The project's only site is
> feelingthemachine.com, and it will never ask you for a password, a payment,
> an ID document, or to download anything. If you'd rather not click a link,
> just reply to this email and I'll add you.
>
> From February 2027 I'll be recording oral history conversations with panel
> members who want to take part: 60–90 minutes, every year or two, about your
> own path into AI and the moments that changed how you see it. Never about
> unreleased products or anything internal to your employer. You would review
> the transcript and decide whether your name is used.
>
> One more ask: if a friend or colleague who works in AI comes to mind, in
> research, engineering, product, data or leadership, would you forward this
> email to them? Please pass it on rather than sending me their details, so
> that each person decides for themselves whether to take part.
>
> Thank you either way, and just reply if you have questions.
>
> Erick
>
> Erick Watson
> Independent researcher, Feeling the Machine · https://feelingthemachine.com

## 2. LinkedIn: connection request note

190 characters with `[Name]`. LinkedIn caps notes at 200 on a free account.
A long first name can push it over, so check before sending.

> Hi [Name], I'm starting Feeling the Machine, a long-term oral history of how
> people who build AI experience its growing abilities. I'd value your
> perspective and would love to tell you more.

After they accept, send message 3.
