This page says what Feeling the Machine collects about you, why, where it is kept, who can see it, and how you can see, change or delete it. Questions go to hello@feelingthemachine.com.本页说明《感受机器》会收集你的哪些信息、为什么收集、保存在哪里、谁能看到，以及你如何查看、修改或删除这些信息。如有疑问，请写信至 hello@feelingthemachine.com。

01Who is responsible责任人

The project is run by Erick Watson, an independent researcher. It is not yet part of a university. Erick decides how your information is used and is responsible for it (in data-protection law, the "controller"). You can reach him at hello@feelingthemachine.com, in English or Chinese.本项目由独立研究者 Erick Watson 负责，目前尚未隶属于任何大学。Erick 决定如何使用你的信息并对此负责（在数据保护法中称为“控制者”或“个人信息处理者”）。你可以用中文或英文写信至 hello@feelingthemachine.com 与他联系。

02This website本网站

The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. If you choose a language, that choice is saved in your own browser and never sent to us.本网站不设置 Cookie，也不使用任何统计分析或跟踪工具。你选择的语言只保存在你自己的浏览器中，不会发送给我们。

Cloudflare hosts the website. Like any web host, it handles your IP address and the details of each request to deliver the page and protect it from attack. We do not receive or keep those records. Everything else the page needs, fonts included, comes from this website itself, so no other company learns that you visited.本网站由 Cloudflare 托管。和所有网站托管服务一样，它会处理你的IP地址和每次访问的请求信息，以便传送网页并防范攻击；我们不会接收或保存这些记录。网页所需的其他一切（包括字体）都来自本网站自身，因此不会有其他公司知道你访问过本网站。

03Joining the panel加入联系名单

Today you join by email. Mail sent to any address at feelingthemachine.com is forwarded by Cloudflare to the researcher's own mailbox. Once your details are safely in the encrypted archive (see section 07), and that has been checked, the emails themselves are deleted from the mailbox. Once you have read and agreed to the panel agreement, we record:目前通过电子邮件加入。发往 feelingthemachine.com 任何地址的邮件都会由 Cloudflare 转发到研究者本人的邮箱。你的信息安全存入加密档案（见第07节）并经核实后，邮件本身会从邮箱中删除。在你阅读并同意联系名单协议之后，我们会记录：

  • your name, email address and, if you give one, a messaging ID;你的姓名、邮箱地址，以及你愿意提供的即时通讯账号；
  • your country, the kind of role you have, and an age band;你所在的国家、你的职位类型和年龄段；
  • what you agreed to (oral history, future studies, updates), which version of the agreement, when, and your preferred language.你同意了哪些事项（口述历史、未来研究、项目进展通知）、协议的版本和日期，以及你偏好的语言。

Nothing is recorded until you agree, and nothing is asked about your health or other sensitive matters.在你同意之前不会记录任何信息，也不会询问你的健康状况或其他敏感信息。

04Signing up on this site在本网站注册

Not open yet尚未开放

Soon you will be able to join and manage your choices here yourself. This section describes how that will work, and takes effect on the day sign-up opens. We will change the date at the top of this page when it does.很快你就可以在本网站自行加入并管理自己的选择。本节说明届时的处理方式，自注册功能开放之日起生效；届时我们会更新本页顶部的日期。

  • Your name and email address are encrypted in your browser before they are sent. Only the researcher's offline, encrypted archive holds the key that can read them. The website cannot.你的姓名和邮箱会先在你的浏览器里加密再发送。只有研究者离线保存的加密档案持有解密密钥，网站本身无法读取。
  • The website knows you by a participant number (such as P012). Under it, it keeps your choices, the history of your consent, and the country, kind of work and age band you gave.网站只通过参与者编号（例如 P012）识别你，并以此编号保存你的选择、同意记录，以及你填写的地区、工作类型和年龄段。
  • You sign in with a link sent to your email; there are no passwords. To send it, Cloudflare handles your address for that moment. The website then keeps only a one-way fingerprint of it, which cannot be turned back into the address.登录时我们会向你的邮箱发送一个登录链接，不设密码。发送时 Cloudflare 会在那一刻处理你的邮箱地址；之后网站只保留一个无法还原成邮箱地址的单向“指纹”。
  • The researcher regularly copies new records into the encrypted archive and then deletes from the website what it no longer needs.研究者会定期把新记录复制到加密档案中，然后从网站上删除不再需要的内容。
  • Signed in, you can see everything the website holds about you, change any choice, download it all, withdraw, or delete your account. Deleting removes your record from the website at once, leaving only your number, the request and its date, so that the archive can carry it out.登录后，你可以查看网站保存的关于你的全部信息、修改任何选择、下载全部数据、退出研究或删除账户。删除会立即从网站上移除你的记录，只保留你的编号、请求内容和日期，以便在档案中执行你的请求。

05Interviews and studies访谈与研究

Oral history recordings and transcripts never pass through this website. They are covered by the information sheet and release form you receive before an interview, which say how they are kept and who may see them. Any formal study will run under a partner institution's ethics approval, with its own consent form and privacy notice.口述历史的录音和文字稿从不经过本网站。它们受访谈前你收到的信息说明和授权书约束，其中写明了保存方式和可查阅的人。任何正式研究都将在合作机构的伦理审批下进行，并使用其自己的知情同意书和隐私声明。

06Why we may use it处理依据

Only because you agreed. You can withdraw that agreement at any time, without giving a reason, and it changes nothing between us. Withdrawing does not undo what was done lawfully before it.我们处理你的信息的唯一依据是你的同意。你可以随时撤回同意，无需说明理由，也不会影响我们之间的关系。撤回同意不影响撤回之前已合法进行的处理。

07Where it is kept, and who sees it保存地点与查阅人员

Your details are kept in an encrypted archive that only the researcher can open, filed under your participant number rather than your name. The list that links numbers to names is kept in a second encrypted archive with a different password. Backups are encrypted too.你的信息保存在只有研究者能打开的加密档案中，以参与者编号而不是姓名归档。编号与姓名的对应表另存于第二个加密档案，使用不同的密码。备份同样加密。

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone outside the project. The only other company involved is Cloudflare, for hosting the website and forwarding email.我们绝不出售你的信息，也不会与项目以外的任何人分享。涉及的其他公司只有 Cloudflare 一家，负责网站托管和邮件转发。

08If you live in China如果你在中国

Your information is stored outside mainland China, by a researcher based in the United States and, from 2027, in Germany. Because that is a transfer abroad, we ask for your separate consent to it before recording anything, and you may refuse. We do not collect health or other sensitive personal information from participants in China.你的信息将保存在中国大陆境外，由常驻美国、自2027年起常驻德国的研究者保管。由于这属于向境外提供个人信息，我们会在记录任何信息之前单独征得你的同意，你也可以拒绝。我们不会向中国境内的参与者收集健康状况或其他敏感个人信息。

09How long it is kept保存期限

Your contact details are deleted 12 months after you leave the panel. The record of what you agreed to, and when, is kept for as long as any material it covers, because it is how we show you agreed. Oral history material is kept as its release form says.你离开联系名单12个月后，你的联系方式将被删除。你同意了什么、何时同意的记录，会与其所涉及的资料保存同样长的时间，因为这是证明你曾同意的依据。口述历史资料按授权书的约定保存。

10Your rights你的权利

You can ask to see everything we hold about you, to correct it, to have a copy, to have it deleted, or to stop any use of it. Write to hello@feelingthemachine.com. We act within 14 days and confirm in writing.你可以要求查看我们保存的关于你的全部信息，要求更正、获取副本、删除，或停止任何使用。请写信至 hello@feelingthemachine.com。我们会在14天内处理并书面确认。

If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can also complain to the data-protection authority where you live.如果你对信息的处理方式不满意，也可以向你所在地的数据保护或个人信息保护主管部门投诉。
