What does it feel like to build a mind that may outgrow your own? 亲手构建一个可能超越自己的智能，是什么感受？

Feeling the Machine follows people who build AI over several years, recording how its growing abilities move them: wonder, unease, and what it does to their sense of who they are. The Chinese word 敬畏 holds both at once: reverence and fear. 《感受机器》将在数年间长期跟踪AI的构建者，记录AI日益强大的能力如何触动他们：惊叹、不安，以及它如何改变人们对“我是谁”的理解。“敬畏”一词恰好同时包含了这两层意思：敬与畏。

01The question研究问题

Psychologists describe awe as the feeling of meeting something vast that our understanding has to stretch to hold. Some awe is uplifting; some is laced with threat. Capable AI may be the first vast thing many people meet in their own line of work.心理学家把“敬畏”描述为面对宏大事物、不得不扩展自身理解时的感受。有的敬畏令人振奋，有的则夹杂着威胁感。对许多人来说，能力强大的AI可能是他们在自己专业领域里第一次遇到的“宏大之物”。

This project asks which kind of awe AI evokes in the people closest to it, in the United States and in China, and whether that feeling leads towards growth or distress over time. It begins as an oral history and is designed to grow into a formal study in cognitive science.本项目关注：AI在离它最近的人身上（在美国和中国）唤起的是哪一种敬畏？随着时间推移，这种感受是促人成长，还是带来困扰？项目以口述历史开始，并将逐步发展为一项认知科学的正式研究。

07Questions常见问题

Who can take part?谁可以参加？

Anyone who builds AI: researchers, engineers, product and data people, and leaders, at any career stage, in the US, China or elsewhere.所有参与构建AI的人：研究员、工程师、产品和数据人员以及管理者，不限职业阶段，不限地区（美国、中国或其他地方）。

Is it paid?有报酬吗？

No. Each year you'll receive a short summary of what the project is learning.没有。每年你会收到一份项目进展的简短总结。

Which language are interviews in?访谈用什么语言？

English or Chinese, whichever you prefer.中文或英文，由你选择。

I live in China. Will my interview leave the country?我在中国，访谈资料会传到境外吗？

Recordings are stored outside mainland China, so the release form asks for your separate consent to that transfer. Interviews in China avoid health and other sensitive topics.录音将保存在中国大陆境外，因此授权书会单独征求你对跨境传输的同意。在中国进行的访谈不涉及健康等敏感话题。

How is my information protected?我的信息如何保护？

Your record is yours: you decide what is kept, and you can take it back. Contact details and recordings are kept encrypted and apart from each other, and filed under a participant number rather than your name. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone outside the project.你的记录属于你自己：保留什么由你决定，你也可以随时收回。联系方式和录音都加密保存、彼此分开，并以参与者编号而不是姓名归档。绝不出售，也不会与项目以外的任何人分享。
