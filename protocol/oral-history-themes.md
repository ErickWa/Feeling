# Oral history themes (v1.0)

Life-story conversations, not a questionnaire. Follow the person's story and order; don't score answers. The hypothesis-driven probes in the Pilot Interview Guide belong to the later formal study.

## Opening (record this)

- Restate the purpose: a historical record of how this period feels to people inside AI.
- Confidentiality: own experiences and feelings only, no unreleased products or internal information.
- They can skip any question, pause, or stop. Ask: "Are you happy to begin?"

## Themes (60 to 90 minutes)

| Theme | English prompt | 中文提示 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path | How did you come to work in AI? What did you think it would become? | 你是怎么走进AI领域的？当时你觉得它会发展成什么样？ |
| Turning points | Tell me about a moment when a system did something that changed how you saw the work, or yourself. | 讲讲某个时刻：一个系统做了某件事，改变了你对工作或对自己的看法。 |
| The feeling | What was that like, in the moment and afterwards? What words would you use, in either language? | 那一刻和之后是什么感受？你会用什么词来形容（中英文都可以）？ |
| Self and work | How has your sense of what you're good for, or who you are, shifted? What do you hold on to? | 你对自己的价值或“我是谁”的认识有什么变化？你坚守的是什么？ |
| Others | How do family, friends and colleagues outside AI react to what you do? | 家人、朋友和AI圈外的同事如何看待你的工作？ |
| Looking ahead | What do you hope for, and what do you fear, for yourself and for others? | 对自己、对他人，你有什么期望，又有什么担忧？ |
| Reflection | What would you want someone reading this in fifty years to understand? | 你希望五十年后读到这段访谈的人理解什么？ |

## Closing

- "Is there anything I didn't ask that you'd like on the record?"
- Explain the transcript review (30 days) and the access choices. Sign Form 3.
