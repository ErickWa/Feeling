# References

The research the project's framing draws on. Each DOI was checked against
Crossref on 2026-11-25.

## The research

1. **Keltner, D., & Haidt, J. (2003).** Approaching awe, a moral, spiritual, and
   aesthetic emotion. *Cognition and Emotion*, 17(2), 297–314.
   https://doi.org/10.1080/02699930302297
   The definition the website uses: awe as perceived *vastness* together with a
   *need for accommodation*, where understanding has to stretch to take
   something in.

2. **Gordon, A. M., Stellar, J. E., Anderson, C. L., McNeil, G. D., Loew, D., &
   Keltner, D. (2017).** The dark side of the sublime: Distinguishing a
   threat-based variant of awe. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*,
   113(2), 310–328. https://doi.org/10.1037/pspp0000120
   Separates positive awe from threat-based awe and finds that the threat-based
   kind brings no gain in well-being. This is the project's central question:
   which kind of awe AI evokes, and whether it leads to growth or distress.

3. **Piff, P. K., Dietze, P., Feinberg, M., Stancato, D. M., & Keltner, D.
   (2015).** Awe, the small self, and prosocial behavior. *Journal of
   Personality and Social Psychology*, 108(6), 883–899.
   https://doi.org/10.1037/pspi0000018
   The "small self": awe shrinks the sense of one's own importance. It is behind
   the *Self and work* theme in `oral-history-themes.md`. The mechanism is
   still debated, so the project treats it as a question rather than a premise.

4. **Yaden, D. B., Kaufman, S. B., Hyde, E., Chirico, A., Gaggioli, A., Zhang,
   J. W., & Keltner, D. (2019).** The development of the Awe Experience Scale
   (AWE-S): A multifactorial measure for a complex emotion. *The Journal of
   Positive Psychology*, 14(4), 474–488.
   https://doi.org/10.1080/17439760.2018.1484940
   A six-factor state measure of awe. It is a candidate instrument for the later
   formal study and is **not** used in the oral history, which scores nothing.

## For a general reader

- **Keltner, D. (2023).** *Awe: The New Science of Everyday Wonder and How It
  Can Transform Your Life.* Penguin Press. Published in the UK as *Awe: The
  Transformative Power of Everyday Wonder*. No Chinese or German edition was
  found on 2026-11-25, so the website cites the English edition in all three
  languages.
